>> This is a simple tutorial the how you can do a mental calculates, there are some tips bellow.

Tip 1

Sums

Example: 35+47 = ? 

                
 - 35 = 30 + 5

                
 - 47 = 40 + 7

                
 - 35 + 47 = 30 + 5 + 40 + 7

                
 - 30 + 40 = 70

                
 - 5 + 7 = 12

                
 - 70 + 12 = 82

                
 35+47 = 82

Tip 2

Multiplication

Example: 9 x 18 = ? 

                
 - 9 x 18 = 9 x (10 + 8)

                
 - 9 x (10 + 8) = 9X10 + 9x8

                
 - 9x10 = 90

                
 - 9x8 = 72

                
 - 90 + 72 = 162

                
 9 x 18 = 162

Tip 3

Example: 15² = ? 25² = ? 35² = ? 

                
 - 15² = __ __ __ ( Three spaces )

                
 - 15² = __ 2 5 (Second and third space aways gonna be 2 and 5)

                
 - 15² =  (1 x 2) 2 5 ( The first number is 1 times your successor)

                
 - 15² = 225

                
 - 25² = (2 x 3) 2 5 

                
 - 25² = 625

                
 - 35² = (3 x 4) 2 5 

                
 - 35² = 1225

Tip 4

Any Number times eleven

Example: 34 x 11 = ? 22 x 11 = ? 67 x 11 = ?

                
34 x 11 = _ _ _ (Three spaces)

                
34 x 11 = 3 _ 4 (First space and third space is always gonna be 3 and 4)

                
34 x 11 = 3 (3+4) 4 (The second space is gonna be 3+4)

                
34 x 11 = 374

                
22 x 11 = 2 (2+2) 2 

                
22 x 11 = 242

                
67 x 11 = 6 (6+7) 7 

                
67 x 11 = (6+1) 3 7 

                
67 x 11 = 737
